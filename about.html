Meet Alexandra

Dedication. Expertise. Results.

Alexandra Kerr

REALTOR® · Estates Director · Senior Real Estate Specialist

(310) 795-1440 alexandra.kerr@compass.com @alexandrakerrlarealestate

The three qualities that distinguish Alexandra as a real estate agent are her dedication, thorough knowledge of the L.A. marketplace, and her efficiency. Regardless of circumstances, she successfully gets the job done for her clients.

Alexandra started her real estate career as a member of one of the most successful, high-end real estate teams in Los Angeles. She quickly rose to become one of the team’s top producers.

Alexandra’s sharp negotiating skills, and her ability to find the best course of action to meet a client’s needs, are the keys to her success. When it comes to the escrow process, she treats each and every transaction with unrivaled attention to detail. Regardless of how challenging the circumstances are, she finds solutions and achieves the best possible results for her clients.

Whether a client is selling or buying a home, Alexandra will get the best price and find the most suitable property. She offers a skilled and personalized approach to the real estate process, making it seamless, efficient, and always successful.
