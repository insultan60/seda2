Selling Your Home

Attracting the highest possible value for your home requires proper preparation, effective marketing and an expert knowledge of local market conditions. I can help you navigate the process and ensure your experience is as positive as it is successful.

Download The Guide

Step by Step

Eight steps to sold.

  1. 01

    Evaluating Your Home

    We’ll evaluate the condition of your home, address any cosmetic or functional problems, and discuss potential upgrades. Some problems may require disclosure to prospective buyers.

  2. 02

    Choosing An Asking Price

    Price is shaped by location, condition, age, and local supply and demand. We’ll conduct a comparative market analysis of similar recent sales — with a professional appraisal as an option — to position your home correctly from day one.

  3. 03

    Getting Market Ready

    Maintenance, repairs, renovations, and staging. We’ll reduce clutter, update paint, and address landscaping so your home shows at its absolute best.

    Get a Home Evaluation
  4. 04

    Listing Your Home

    Professional photography, an MLS listing with broad syndication, and a personalized marketing plan built around your home’s strengths.

  5. 05

    Home Showings

    Scheduled showings and open houses bring qualified buyers through the door. As the homeowner you typically won’t be present, and you’ll always receive advance notification.

  6. 06

    Receiving Offers

    Accept, counter, or reject — we’ll negotiate price, concessions, and financing terms together, including strategy for multiple-offer situations.

  7. 07

    Appraisal & Inspection

    The buyer orders an inspection and their lender orders an appraisal. Additional negotiation on repairs may follow — I’ll manage it on your behalf.

  8. 08

    Closing

    The closing disclosure is issued, paperwork is signed, and proceeds are wired to your bank account. Your home is officially sold.

The Staging Checklist

Room by room, everything your home needs before the photographer arrives.

The Basics
  • Organize and declutter every room.
  • Empty the garage, attic, and closets.
  • Deep clean the entire home.
  • Remove personal items.
  • Replace dead light bulbs.
  • Open draperies and let the light in.
  • Check that doors, cabinets, and windows open smoothly.
Living Areas
  • Remove unnecessary furniture.
  • Use lamps for warm lighting.
  • Clean the fireplace.
  • Style with coordinated accessories.
Kitchen
  • Clear the countertops.
  • Empty and organize cabinets.
  • Clean appliances inside and out.
  • Organize the pantry.
  • Remove refrigerator magnets.
Bathrooms
  • Scrub every surface.
  • Remove personal products.
  • Replace shower curtains and mats.
  • Set out neutral towels.
  • Check caulk and grout.
Bedrooms
  • Remove excess clothing from closets.
  • Store valuables securely.
  • Depersonalize the space.
  • Remove game consoles.
  • Choose gender-neutral styling.
Walls, Windows & Doors
  • Remove personal items from walls.
  • Patch holes.
  • Repaint bold colors with neutrals.
  • Wash windows.
  • Repair screens.
Exterior
  • Power-wash siding and walkways.
  • Mow and edge the lawn.
  • Trim trees.
  • Clean gutters.
  • Repaint the front door.
  • Add potted plants.
